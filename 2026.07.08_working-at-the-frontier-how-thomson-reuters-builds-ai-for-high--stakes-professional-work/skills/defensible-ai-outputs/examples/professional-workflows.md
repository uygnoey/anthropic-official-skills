# Professional workflows from the Thomson Reuters post

## Legal research, rebuilt around verification

Thomson Reuters rebuilt legal research around agents tuned for **citation validation and verification**
rather than just search and retrieval.

The point of the change is what the professional does next. With findings whose citations have already
been checked, professionals review, verify, and apply judgment with confidence — rather than starting
from scratch on whether the cited authority says what the finding claims.

The products underneath: Westlaw and Practical Law for legal research, and CoCounsel Legal, a
professional-grade legal AI platform designed to make legal professionals more effective with
defensible answers.

## CoCounsel Legal, rebuilt on the Claude Agent SDK

**Before.** Separate skills running sequentially.

**After.** Rebuilt on the Claude Agent SDK, planning and orchestrating across tools in real time.

A single agent now accesses hundreds of company tools simultaneously. Customer data remains protected
and is not used for third-party model training.

Hron's test for a model, given this architecture:

> "Our big test for Claude is to assess how good it is at making plans and using tools effectively."

## Advanced drafting

The capability-expansion requirement, concretely: motion drafting and filings that professionals would
"spend days or weeks perfecting."

This is the class of work where the four requirements compound. A motion draft needs valid citations,
needs the thread of the matter held across a long chain of tool calls, needs the attorney involved in
developing it rather than reviewing a finished artifact, and needs drafting capability beyond
summarization.

## Internal error remediation

An internal error-remediation tool built on Claude reduced root cause analysis **from three hours to
four minutes**.

This is one of the measures Thomson Reuters still tracks alongside engineering measures like DORA and
time-to-production — even though Hron cautions against over-indexing on rate-of-return calculations.

## The standard the work is held to

> For work that "ultimately has to hold up in court," professional AI has to work in environments where
> "being almost right is not good enough."
