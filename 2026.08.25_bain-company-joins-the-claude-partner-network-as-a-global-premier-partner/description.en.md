## What is this post?
An announcement that Bain & Company has joined the Claude Partner Network as a Global Premier partner, with client work organized around AI strategy, technology modernization, and AI-enabled operations.

The reusable material is the internal deployment described inside the announcement. Bain rolled Claude out to all 19,000 of its employees across five surfaces, ran a pilot that produced an active-use number rather than a seat count, and shipped an enablement and governance wrapper alongside the launch.

## When is it useful?
- When planning a firm-wide AI assistant deployment and deciding which surfaces to deploy rather than which single tool to standardize on.
- When a pilot needs to be turned into a defensible general-availability decision.
- When adoption has stalled after licences went out and the pilot never produced a real number.
- When choosing the first serious workstream to point AI capability at.
- When budgeting a rollout and the deployment expertise has been left out of the plan.

## Key points
- **A surface set, not a tool.** Claude.ai, Claude Cowork, Claude Code, Claude for Excel, and Claude for Microsoft 365. Three of the five put the model inside a tool the population already had open.
- **The role-native surface converts.** More than two-thirds of pilot participants adopted Claude for Excel — the only per-surface number reported, and the surface embedded where a consulting firm's analytical work actually happens.
- **The metric is active use over a short window.** More than 7,000 employees actively using Claude within weeks of the firm-wide deployment — a statement about uptake velocity, not about accounts provisioned.
- **Five enablement components, shipped with the launch.** Onboarding materials, training sessions, webinars, expert support, and governance structures, with user experience tracked through ongoing surveys and feedback collection.
- **The client target was chosen deliberately.** Productivity gains of 30% to 50% across engagements involving complex legacy codebases that lack architectural context — work where the bottleneck is recovering lost understanding rather than raw volume.
- **Deployment expertise is the scarce input.** Bain's digital teams comprise more than 1,500 AI, data, analytics, architecture, and engineering experts working alongside its industry and capability practices. Anthropic's Steve Corfield read the speed and breadth of adoption as a signal of what happens when technology is paired with the expertise to deploy it.
- **Three partnership focus areas** — AI strategy, technology modernization, AI-enabled operations — with collaboration underway across multiple industries and plans to expand.
