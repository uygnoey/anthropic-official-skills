# Worked example: the reported Bain rollout

Everything below is what the source article reports. Where a step of the method is being
illustrated rather than quoted, it is marked as a reading.

## The surface set

Claude was rolled out to all 19,000 employees across five surfaces:

| Surface | Population it reaches |
|---|---|
| Claude.ai | General knowledge work, firm-wide |
| Claude Cowork | Agentic delivery work |
| Claude Code | Engineering |
| Claude for Excel | Modelling and analysis |
| Claude for Microsoft 365 | Documents and mail, firm-wide |

**Reading:** three of the five sit inside a tool the population already had open. That is
the pattern the method's step 1 generalizes — meet the role where it works rather than
asking it to come to a new window.

## The pilot number

More than 7,000 employees were **actively using** Claude within weeks of the firm-wide
deployment. Anthropic's Steve Corfield, Global Head of Business Development, described the
speed and breadth of that adoption as a signal of what is possible when the right technology
is paired with the expertise to deploy it effectively.

More than two-thirds of pilot participants adopted Claude for Excel.

**Reading:** the headline metric is active use over a short, stated window — not seats
provisioned, not licences issued. The Excel figure is the only per-surface number reported,
and it is the most role-native surface in the set.

## The enablement wrapper

The implementation included:

- Onboarding materials
- Training sessions
- Webinars
- Expert support
- Governance structures

User experience was tracked through ongoing surveys and feedback collection.

## The partnership shape

Bain joined the Claude Partner Network as a Global Premier partner. Client work is organized
around three areas:

1. AI strategy
2. Technology modernization
3. AI-enabled operations

Bain's digital teams comprise more than 1,500 AI, data, analytics, architecture, and
engineering experts, working alongside its industry and capability practices.

Philippe d'Arabian, EVP and Global Head of Partnerships at Bain, framed the partnership as
combining Anthropic's frontier AI technology with Bain's strategic and industry expertise to
turn AI potential into business results.

## The client outcome

Across multiple engagements involving complex legacy codebases — specifically codebases
lacking architectural context — clients saw productivity gains of **30% to 50%**.

**Reading:** the target was chosen for a reason. The binding constraint on that work is
recovering understanding nobody holds any more, which is a different problem from doing more
of something already understood. Step 5 of the method is this choice generalized.

The firms are collaborating on client engagements across multiple industries, with plans to
expand further.
