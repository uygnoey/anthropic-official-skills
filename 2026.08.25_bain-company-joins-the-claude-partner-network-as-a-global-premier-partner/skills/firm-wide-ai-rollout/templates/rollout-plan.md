## Enablement wrapper

Each must exist before general availability, in this order.

- [ ] Governance structures — data rules per surface, client-work approval, ownership,
      escalation path
- [ ] Onboarding materials — one page per surface, role-specific first task
- [ ] Training sessions — role-segmented, hands-on, scheduled per cohort
- [ ] Webinars — launch session plus recurring cadence, recorded
- [ ] Expert support — staffed channel, routed by surface, feeding gaps back weekly
- [ ] Surveys and feedback — recurring, not launch-only
