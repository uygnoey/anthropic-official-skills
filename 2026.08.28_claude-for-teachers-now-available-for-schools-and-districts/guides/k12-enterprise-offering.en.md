# Claude for Teachers as a district-wide offering

Claude for Teachers is available to schools and districts as a **free Enterprise offering**.
Qualifying organizations that register by **June 30, 2027** receive one year of complimentary
access.

## What the district-level version adds

Individual teachers could already verify and use Claude for Teachers. Moving to a centrally managed
account changes what the district can do around it:

- **Centrally managed accounts** with enterprise features, including **single sign-on** and
  **role-based access controls**.
- **Free access for educators** to capabilities designed for K-12 instruction.
- **Teaching skills aligned with state academic standards across all 50 states.**
- **FERPA compliance** maintained through district-level terms and data processing agreements.

## Setup, in four steps

1. **Verify the district.**
2. **Accept the K-12 terms.**
3. **Connect the email domain.**
4. **Connect single sign-on.**

Teachers **already verified on the district domain transfer automatically** to the centralized
account. There is no re-registration step for them — say so plainly in the rollout communication,
because that is where domain migrations usually generate confusion.

## Student data

- Student data **is not used for model training purposes**.
- Student information **is protected by the K-12 Data Processing Agreement**.

Bring both statements to the district's privacy reviewer at the start of the process rather than at
the end. They are the questions that stall an otherwise approved rollout.

## What shipped for back to school

Two new teaching skills:

- **Lesson Preparation** — assists educators in planning key instructional moments.
- **Check for Understanding** — creates standards-aligned assessments, **initially available for
  mathematics**.

Plus enhanced accessibility for student materials and updated lesson planning functionality.

## Sequencing a rollout

- **Choose the first cohort by standards coverage.** Check for Understanding starts with
  mathematics, so a math department can use both new skills immediately; other departments start
  with Lesson Preparation, which is subject-independent.
- **Introduce skills, not a product.** "Assessments that map to our state standards" lands
  differently from "an AI assistant."
- **Finish verification and SSO before the registration deadline.** June 30, 2027 governs
  registration, not usage.
