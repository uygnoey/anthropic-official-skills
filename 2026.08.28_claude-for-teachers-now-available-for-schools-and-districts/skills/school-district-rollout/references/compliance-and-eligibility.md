# Eligibility, terms, and student data

What to hand to whoever owns privacy and procurement review in the district. Everything here is as
stated in the source announcement; anything a reviewer asks beyond this needs to go back to the
official K-12 terms.

## Eligibility and cost

- **Who:** K-12 schools and districts.
- **What:** Claude for Teachers as a **free Enterprise offering**.
- **Deadline:** qualifying organizations that **register by June 30, 2027** receive **one year of
  complimentary access**.

The deadline governs registration, not usage. Plan the verification and SSO work to complete before
it.

## Terms and agreements

- **K-12 terms** are accepted at the district level, not per teacher.
- A **K-12 Data Processing Agreement** covers student information.
- **FERPA compliance** is maintained through those district-level terms and the data processing
  agreement.

## Student data

- Student data **is not used for model training purposes**.
- Student information **is protected by the K-12 Data Processing Agreement**.

These are the two statements a privacy review usually needs in writing before it can proceed.

## Enterprise controls the district gains

Centrally managed accounts bring enterprise features:

- **Single sign-on.**
- **Role-based access controls.**

Both arrive with the centralized account rather than with individual teacher verification — which is
the practical argument for making the move even where teachers are already using it successfully.

## Setup sequence

1. Verify the district.
2. Accept the K-12 terms.
3. Connect the email domain.
4. Connect single sign-on.

## Migration of existing teachers

Teachers **already verified on the district domain transfer automatically** to the centralized
account. No re-registration step exists for them, and none should be communicated.
