# Claude for Teachers is now available for schools and districts

## What is this post?

An announcement: Claude for Teachers is available to schools and districts as a free Enterprise
offering, with one year of complimentary access for qualifying organizations that register by
June 30, 2027. The post covers what the district-level version adds — centrally managed accounts,
single sign-on, role-based access controls, FERPA compliance through district-level terms — the
four-step setup, how already-verified teachers migrate, and two new teaching skills shipped for
back to school.

## When is it useful?

- A district is moving from individually verified teacher accounts to a centrally managed one.
- Privacy or procurement review needs the student-data and FERPA position in writing.
- You are planning which departments or capabilities to roll out first.
- You need to know the registration deadline and what it actually governs.

## Key points

- **Free Enterprise offering** for K-12 schools and districts; **register by June 30, 2027** for one
  year of complimentary access.
- **Centrally managed accounts** bring enterprise features including **single sign-on** and
  **role-based access controls**.
- **Free access for educators** to capabilities designed for K-12 instruction.
- **Teaching skills aligned with state academic standards across all 50 states.**
- **FERPA compliance** maintained through district-level terms and data processing agreements.
- **Setup:** verify the district, accept the K-12 terms, connect the email domain, connect single
  sign-on.
- **Teachers already verified on the district domain transfer automatically** to the centralized
  account.
- **Student data is not used for model training**, and student information is protected by the K-12
  Data Processing Agreement.
- **Two new teaching skills:** *Lesson Preparation*, which assists educators in planning key
  instructional moments, and *Check for Understanding*, which creates standards-aligned assessments
  and is initially available for mathematics.
- **Also shipped:** enhanced accessibility for student materials and updated lesson planning
  functionality.
